1. Bundled World Bank GDP snapshot

world_bank_gdp_current_usd_2000_2024.csv contains observations of the World
Development Indicators series GDP (current US$) (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD) for
2000–2024. It was obtained through the World Bank API and incorporated into
SENSE on 2026-09-03.

Source: World Bank national accounts data and OECD National Accounts data
files, distributed through the World Bank Open Data API:
https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD

The World Bank: World Development Indicators: GDP (current US$), indicator
NY.GDP.MKTP.CD. The indicator page explicitly identifies CC BY 4.0:
https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Additional World Bank terms: https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use
Retain this attribution and those terms when redistributing the snapshot.
The bundled CSV selects and reformats API observations for 2000-2024;
SENSE-derived weights are not original World Bank observations.

SENSE uses these values only to calculate relative country GDP shares. The
weights and resulting SENSE outputs are transformations produced by SENSE;
they are not official World Bank products. No endorsement by the World Bank is
implied.

2. Bundled monetary constants

src/sense/monetary.py contains selected euro-area HICP annual-average indices
from Eurostat (PRC_HICP_AIND) and annual-average USD/EUR reference rates from
the European Central Bank. Data accessed 2026-09-03.

Eurostat source: https://doi.org/10.2908/PRC_HICP_AIND
Reuse terms: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice
The indices used here are for the euro area. SENSE calculates index ratios
and currency-conversion factors; these transformations are SENSE's responsibility,
not Eurostat's. Eurostat bears no responsibility for this use or interpretation.

ECB source: https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/EXR
Copyright and reuse terms:
https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html
The original rate information is available free of charge from the ECB.
If ECB information is incorporated in documents sold to users, the publisher
must tell buyers that the information is available free from the ECB, before
payment and each time they access it. Modifications must be identified.
SENSE combines these rates with HICP ratios to calculate its own conversion
factors. The ECB is the source of the rates, not the calculated SENSE factors.
Retain source attribution and the applicable reuse conditions.

3. Public methodology references

The default risk/opportunity scale is implemented as model settings, not as
country-sector assessments. The public PSILCA v3 manual, section 3.8, Table 3,
describes exponential level factors and the no-data factor:
https://psilca.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/PSILCA_documentation_v3.pdf
SENSE assigns 0.001 to "no" to retain positivity for geometric averaging; the
manual assigns zero. This deviation is a SENSE modelling choice, not a copied
sector observation. No manual or assessment table is redistributed.

4. Downloaded or user-provided inputs (not bundled)

The default BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 embedding model is downloaded at runtime.
Its model card identifies the MIT licence:
https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5
The hydrogen foreground stage separately downloads
sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, whose model card identifies Apache
License 2.0: https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
The pinned model revisions are recorded in src/sense/data/embeddings.py.
Model weights are not included in SENSE distributions.

The optional hydrogen example downloads Regioinvent trade data from the
provider's pinned record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20596474
The record declares CC BY 4.0. That database is not redistributed with SENSE.
Consult the record and the original providers' terms before reusing or
redistributing downloaded inputs.

Ecoinvent, PSILCA, SOCA, ecoinvent overview workbooks, and foreground workbooks
(including their study-selection and settings sheets)
must be supplied or obtained by the user under their applicable terms. None
is included in the SENSE 1.1.0 distributions. Separately installed Python
dependencies retain their respective licences.

5. Bundled IMF WEO GDP fallback

Source: International Monetary Fund, World Economic Outlook (April 2026).
https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/NGDPD@WEO
Indicator: NGDPD, GDP at current prices, billions of US dollars.
API data and metadata accessed 2026-09-23.

imf_weo_gdp_current_usd_2000_2024.csv selects individual-economy estimates for
2000-2024, converts billions of US dollars to dollars, and maps IMF UVK to XKX
and WBG to PSE. Regional totals and years after 2024 are excluded. The companion
JSON records source metadata, raw-response hashes, and the transformed CSV hash.
These historical WEO estimates need not be directly measured national observations.
The original World Bank snapshot is unchanged; IMF supplies a value only when
World Bank has none usable within the configured year window.

IMF statistical data are subject to their own terms, not the SENSE MIT licence
or the World Bank CC BY 4.0 licence:
https://www.imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms
The section "The Use of IMF Data" permits distribution and transformation with
accurate source attribution and disclosure of transformations; consult that
section and its conditions, including its direction to contact the IMF for
potential commercial reuse. Communicate these terms when redistributing data.
IMF data are available free from the IMF. The dollar conversion and weights are
SENSE transformations, not official IMF products; no IMF endorsement is implied.

6. Bundled native database reader

resources/zolca/reader.jar contains SENSE's Java reader and the public Apache
Derby engine/shared modules 10.17.1.0. It contains no openLCA application code,
PSILCA database, matrix, assessment or licensed input. The SENSE Java sources
are distributed under the repository MIT licence. Apache Derby is distributed
under Apache License 2.0. Its original LICENSE and NOTICE files are retained
inside the JAR, including copyright and attribution notices.

Public dependency sources:
https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/org/apache/derby/derby/10.17.1.0/
https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/org/apache/derby/derbyshared/10.17.1.0/
Project and source: https://db.apache.org/derby/

Verified upstream SHA-256 values:
derby: 764e4c133f860a8876e835ef2306efecad27a742d27f05bc4bce669432c6b397
derbyshared: fb68bab30785375c1f8cb5e9583b349750f8904d6f7ee15ce8e1c30b6be30ef4

Rebuild with tools/build_zolca_reader.py using Java 21. The script verifies
the public dependencies, compiles the included sources and writes a deterministic
JAR. Normal package installation uses the bundled artifact without downloading
Java code. Java itself is supplied separately, for example by Conda openjdk=21,
and retains its own licence.
